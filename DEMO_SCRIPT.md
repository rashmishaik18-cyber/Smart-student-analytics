# 3-minute demo / slide outline

1. **Problem (20s)**: Attendance, exams, LMS, clubs, placement tests and feedback sit in 10 separate files. Nobody sees a student across all of them, so help arrives late.
2. **Data integration (30s)**: open *Data integration*. 10 sources, IDs standardised, duplicates and unmatched IDs removed, outliers fixed, gaps filled, completeness tracked per student.
3. **The score (30s)**: open *How the score works*. 16 indicators, 7 pillars, weights visible. Risk rules are listed in plain words.
4. **Campus view (40s)**: *Overview*. Point at KPIs, the score distribution, attendance-vs-CGPA scatter (the lower-left cluster is the red zone), risk by department, trends. Read two of the insights aloud.
5. **Segments (20s)**: click "Strong academics, low placement readiness" in the segment chart. These students look fine on grades but are not placement-ready; the action is a bootcamp, not academic coaching.
6. **One student (30s)**: *Intervention list*, sorted by priority. Click the top row. Show reasons, suggested actions, the red/green driver bars and the SGPA trend with forecast.
7. **Close (10s)**: Download the filtered list as CSV for mentors. Next steps: plug in real exports, validate weights against actual outcomes.
