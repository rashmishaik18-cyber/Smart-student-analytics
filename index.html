What the combined data shows

    Ten sources, one student view

    Every source is keyed by student ID, cleaned, and joined onto the academic record. Gaps are filled with the median of the same department and year, and each student keeps a completeness figure.

    Cleaning steps applied

    • IDs such as s0012, S0012 and S-0012 become S0012.
    • Department spellings (Comp Sci, C.S.E, cse) map to one code; year text such as "Year 2" becomes 2.
    • Duplicate rows are removed; rows whose ID is not in the academic record are dropped.
    • CGPA entered on a 100-point scale is converted to 10; small overshoots (attendance 106%) are capped; impossible values (aptitude -1) are treated as missing.

    Student Success Score (0 to 100)

    Each indicator is put on a 0 to 100 scale and multiplied by its weight. The score is the sum.

    Bands: Excellent 75+, Good 60 to 74, Watch 45 to 59, Critical below 45.
